# Awesome Free Tools for Creators

A curated list of genuinely free tools for people who make things — video, audio, design, writing, marketing and more. Every tool here has a usable free tier you can keep using. No trials that expire, no "free" plans that are just demos.

[![Entries](https://img.shields.io/badge/tools-100-brightgreen)](tools.json) [![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/licence-MIT-yellow.svg)](LICENSE) [![PRs Welcome](https://img.shields.io/badge/PRs-welcome-brightgreen.svg)](CONTRIBUTING.md)

**How this list stays honest**

- Every entry names what the free tier actually gives you, in the _Free:_ note. If a plan shrinks, the note gets fixed or the tool gets removed.
- Short free trials do not count. Neither do free plans so limited you cannot finish a real piece of work.
- No affiliate links, ever. Every URL goes straight to the tool itself.
- Entries live in [`tools.json`](tools.json) and this README is generated from it, so the list and the searchable website can never drift apart.

**Browse it as a website:** open the companion site in [`docs/`](docs/) (or the GitHub Pages deployment) to search and filter the whole list.

## Video & Animation

Edit, record, convert and animate video without paying for an editor.

- [Blender](https://www.blender.org) — 3D creation suite with modelling, animation, rendering and a built-in video editor. _Free: Free and open source._
- [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com) — Easy video editor for short-form content, on mobile, desktop and web. _Free: Free plan: full editor with 1080p exports; a watermark only appears if you use Pro-marked templates or effects._
- [Clipchamp](https://clipchamp.com) — Microsoft's beginner-friendly browser and Windows video editor. _Free: Free plan: unlimited exports up to 1080p with no watermark._
- [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) — Professional video editor with colour grading, effects and audio tools in one app. _Free: Free version: nearly the full editor, no watermark and no export limits._
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com) — Edit audio and video by editing the transcript, like a document. _Free: Free plan: 1 hour of transcription per month._
- [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr) — Convert and compress video files into smaller MP4 or WebM files. _Free: Free and open source._
- [Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org) — Open-source editor with multi-track editing and plenty of effects. _Free: Free and open source._
- [LocalMediaKit](https://github.com/MrRandom-0/localmediakit) — Compress, convert, trim and subtitle media files entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded. _Free: Free and open source; every tool runs locally on your device._
- [Loom](https://www.loom.com) — Record quick screen-and-camera videos instead of holding a meeting. _Free: Free plan: up to 25 videos, up to 5 minutes each._
- [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com) — Record your screen and stream live to YouTube, Twitch and more. _Free: Free and open source, with no limits or watermarks._
- [OpenShot](https://www.openshot.org) — Simple open-source video editor for quick cuts, titles and effects. _Free: Free and open source._
- [Shotcut](https://shotcut.org) — Capable open-source editor that handles almost any format without importing. _Free: Free and open source._

## Audio & Music

Record and edit podcasts, voiceovers and music.

- [Adobe Podcast](https://podcast.adobe.com) — Clean up spoken audio and record podcasts in the browser. _Free: Free with an Adobe account: Enhance Speech with usage limits._
- [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org) — Record and edit audio: trim, clean up, mix and add effects. _Free: Free and open source._
- [Auphonic](https://auphonic.com) — Automatically level voices, reduce noise and hit loudness targets for podcasts. _Free: Free plan: 2 hours of processed audio per month (free outputs add a short Auphonic jingle)._
- [BandLab](https://www.bandlab.com) — Make music in your browser with instruments, loops and effects, plus a social platform. _Free: Free: the full studio with unlimited projects._
- [GarageBand](https://www.apple.com/mac/garageband/) — Apple's music studio for recording songs and podcasts on Mac and iOS. _Free: Free with Apple devices._
- [LMMS](https://lmms.io) — Produce music with built-in instruments, samples and a piano roll. _Free: Free and open source._
- [Ocenaudio](https://www.ocenaudio.com) — Fast, lightweight audio editor that is simpler than most full DAWs. _Free: Free to use._
- [Waveform Free](https://www.tracktion.com/products/waveform-free) — A full digital audio workstation with no track-count restrictions. _Free: Free version: the complete DAW, no track limits._

## Design & Images

Thumbnails, graphics, logos, photo editing and illustrations.

- [ASCII Images](https://ascii-images.com) — Creates image-to-ASCII artwork, FIGlet banners, patterns and plain-text diagrams. _Free: Free tier: 18 browser tools with no account or trial; individual tools have documented input limits; site uses Google Analytics._
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com) — Design posts, thumbnails, presentations and more from thousands of templates. _Free: Free plan: large template and element library; some assets are Pro-only._
- [Coolors](https://coolors.co) — Generate and explore colour palettes for your brand and designs. _Free: Free tier available._
- [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com) — Sketch-style whiteboard for diagrams, wireframes and quick explainers. _Free: Free to use in the browser; open source._
- [Figma](https://www.figma.com) — Design interfaces and graphics collaboratively in the browser. _Free: Free Starter plan: 3 shared design files and unlimited personal drafts._
- [GIMP](https://www.gimp.org) — Full photo editor and design tool for retouching, compositing and graphics. _Free: Free and open source._
- [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org) — Vector graphics editor for logos, icons and illustrations (SVG). _Free: Free and open source._
- [Krita](https://krita.org) — Digital painting app made for illustrators, comics and concept art. _Free: Free and open source._
- [Penpot](https://penpot.app) — Open-source design and prototyping tool, self-hostable and browser-based. _Free: Free and open source; the hosted version is free to use._
- [Photopea](https://www.photopea.com) — Photoshop-style editor in the browser that opens PSD, AI and Sketch files. _Free: Free to use, supported by ads._
- [remove.bg](https://www.remove.bg) — Remove the background from a photo in one click. _Free: Free: low-resolution results; full-resolution downloads need credits._
- [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app) — Compress and resize images in the browser and compare settings side by side. _Free: Free, runs fully in your browser._
- [Upscayl](https://upscayl.org) — Enlarge and sharpen images with AI, on your own computer. _Free: Free and open source._

## Writing & Blogging

Draft, check and publish writing — from posts to full newsletters.

- [FocusWriter](https://gottcode.org/focuswriter/) — Distraction-free writing app that hides everything except your words. _Free: Free and open source._
- [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com) — Write, edit and collaborate on documents in your browser. _Free: Free with a Google account._
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com) — Checks spelling, grammar and tone as you type, almost everywhere. _Free: Free plan: core spelling, grammar and punctuation checks._
- [Hashnode](https://hashnode.com) — Blogging platform with a free custom domain, built for developers who write. _Free: Free to blog on your own domain._
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com) — Highlights hard-to-read sentences so your writing gets clearer. _Free: Free in the browser (the desktop app is paid)._
- [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org) — Grammar and style checker for more than 20 languages. _Free: Free plan: basic checking for shorter texts._
- [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org) — Complete free office suite: documents, spreadsheets and presentations. _Free: Free and open source._
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) — Note-taking app that stores your writing as plain Markdown files on your device. _Free: Free for personal use._
- [StackEdit](https://stackedit.io) — Markdown editor in the browser with live preview and cloud sync. _Free: Free to use in the browser._
- [Substack](https://substack.com) — Publish a newsletter and blog, with paid subscriptions built in. _Free: Free to publish; Substack takes a percentage only of paid subscriptions._

## SEO & Marketing

Get found, grow an audience and send email people actually open.

- [Ahrefs Webmaster Tools](https://ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools) — Audit your own website for SEO issues and see your backlinks. _Free: Free for verified site owners._
- [AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com) — Turns real search questions into content ideas, grouped by topic. _Free: Free tier: a limited number of searches per day._
- [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters) — Monitor and improve your site's presence in Bing search. _Free: Free from Microsoft._
- [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com) — Email and SMS marketing, plus a simple CRM. _Free: Free plan: 300 emails per day with large contact storage._
- [Google Analytics](https://analytics.google.com) — Understand where your visitors come from and what they read or watch. _Free: Free from Google._
- [Google Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/) — Check search demand for keywords straight from Google's ad data. _Free: Free with a Google Ads account._
- [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) — See how your site performs in Google search and fix indexing problems. _Free: Free from Google._
- [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com) — Compare what people are searching for, and spot rising topics early. _Free: Free from Google._
- [HubSpot Free Tools](https://www.hubspot.com) — Free CRM with email tracking, forms, live chat and landing pages. _Free: Free CRM tier, no time limit._
- [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com) — Email marketing for small audiences, with landing pages and forms. _Free: Free plan: small contact list with limited monthly sends._
- [Screaming Frog SEO Spider](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/) — Crawl your website to find broken links, duplicate titles and other issues. _Free: Free version crawls up to 500 URLs._
- [Ubersuggest](https://ubersuggest.com) — Keyword ideas, content ideas and basic site audits. _Free: Free tier: a limited number of searches per day._

- [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com) — Schedule Facebook and Instagram posts and reply to messages in one inbox. _Free: Free with a Facebook account._
- [Metricool](https://metricool.com) — Schedule posts and check analytics for most major networks. _Free: Free plan: 1 brand with limited monthly posts._
- [Pinterest Trends](https://trends.pinterest.com) — See what people are searching for and saving on Pinterest. _Free: Free to browse._
- [Social Blade](https://socialblade.com) — Public follower and growth statistics for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and more. _Free: Free stats lookups._
- [TikTok Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter) — Trending songs, hashtags and top ads on TikTok, straight from TikTok. _Free: Free to browse._

## Suggest a tool

Open an issue with the **Suggest a tool** template, or send a pull request that adds one entry to `tools.json`. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) first — the criteria are strict on purpose, and one-tool-per-PR keeps reviews fast.

## Curation rules

A tool belongs on this list only if **all** of these are true:

1. **The free tier is real.** You can finish actual work with it and keep using it indefinitely. Trials, one-off credit packs and demo modes do not count.
2. **The limits are stated honestly.** If exports are watermarked, resolution-capped or credit-limited, the _Free:_ note says so.
3. **It is useful to creators, students or freelancers** — the common person test: would someone making videos, posts, music or a small business plausibly use this weekly?
4. **The link is direct.** Official site only. No affiliate codes, no referral parameters, no link shorteners.
5. **It is still alive.** Actively maintained, reachable, and the free tier still exists (checked monthly by automation — see [MAINTENANCE.md](MAINTENANCE.md)).

Tools are removed when the free tier disappears, the site dies, or the free plan becomes too limited to finish real work.

## Contributing

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). In short: edit only `tools.json`, one tool per pull request, and run `node scripts/build.mjs` — CI will reject a README that is out of sync with `tools.json`.
